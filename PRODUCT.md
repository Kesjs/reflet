# PRODUCT — Reflet

## What is Reflet?

Reflet is an analytics system that measures, analyzes, and tracks brand visibility in ChatGPT-generated responses. It's a "Search Console for AI answers" that explains why a brand appears or doesn't, and verifies changes over time.

**Product Loop:** SEE → UNDERSTAND → ACT → VERIFY

## Audience

**Primary:** French SMBs (Petites et Moyennes Entreprises françaises)
**Type:** B2B analytics product

## Platform

**Web** — Landing page + web application

## Mode

**Persuade** — This is a landing page designed to convert visitors into users

## Tone & Voice

**Analytical, sober, premium, credible**

The product is positioned as an analytical measurement instrument—precise, professional, and trustworthy. Not flashy, not overly promotional. Think of references like:
- **Linear** — Navigation, density, hierarchy, dark-first
- **Vercel** — Sobriety, neutral surfaces, thin borders, sparing use of color
- **Stripe** — Data structuring, metrics, tables, charts
- **PostHog** — Analytics logic, monitoring, data exploration

## Design Constraints

### ❌ EXPLICIT ANTI-PATTERNS TO AVOID

**"AI flashy" generic aesthetics:**
- Omnipresent decorative gradients (purple→blue)
- Luminous glows around buttons/inputs
- Gratuitous neons, halo effects
- Abstract illustrations without product purpose
- Artificial accumulation of cards
- "Pill" style (radius 30px+)
- Purely decorative animations
- "AI-generic" vague promotional copy

### ✅ DESIGN PRINCIPLES

```
HIERARCHY → DATA → CONTEXT → PROOF → ACTION
```

Design organizes space so important information has clear hierarchy—it doesn't fill space to look pretty.

**Typography:** Geist (décidé — voir reflet-brand-tokens.md)
**Brand Color:** Jaune soufre — `--brand-fill: #c9ab1e` (fond CTA) / `--brand-text-accent: #f2d94e` (liens, emphase) / `--on-brand: #0b0b0b` (texte sur fond brand)
**Palette:** Noir profond (`--surface-1: #0b0b0b` / `--surface-2: #111111`) — voir reflet-brand-tokens.md
**Border Radius:** Moderate scale (6px to 16px max), NO pill style
**Animation:** Purposeful only, 60fps, respect prefers-reduced-motion

## Content Guidelines

**Language:** French (primary target market)

**Copy principles:**
- Direct, factual, no hyperbole
- Data-driven, proof-backed
- No AI-generic buzzwords ("seamless", "robust", "empower")
- Clear hierarchy of information
- Professional but accessible

## Success Criteria

- Sober, precise, premium, credible aesthetic
- Clear information hierarchy
- Purposeful use of color (jaune soufre accent uniquement — un seul CTA visible à la fois)
- No decorative gradients or glows
- Real product visuals, not abstract illustrations
- Geist font throughout
- 60fps animations
- WCAG AA accessibility compliance
